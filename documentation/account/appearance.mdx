---
title: "Account and appearance"
description: "Update your profile and choose a comfortable theme."
---

Open **Settings → Account** to update your profile. Change your first name, last name or profile image, then save the profile changes.

## Choose your theme

Under **Appearance**, select System, Light or Dark. Account theme changes apply immediately; there is no separate Save appearance action. System follows your device preference.

Your account theme controls your dashboard experience. Workspace appearance is managed separately in **Settings → Branding**, where changes require **Save changes**.

## Workspace branding

The workspace image and name appear on its public board and widget. Its accent is used by controls and active votes. A Dynamic public-board theme allows light and dark appearance rather than locking one theme.

## Sign-in and account security

Use the sign-in settings control to open your secure Clerk account settings. Account deletion is also reviewed there before you confirm it. Do not send sign-in codes to support.
