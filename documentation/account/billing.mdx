## Start a trial

Eligible workspaces can start a **14-day Essentials trial** from **Settings → Billing**. A card that supports USD payments is required. The trial is available once per account/email and workspace; canceling or deleting a workspace does not make a new trial available. Review the selected interval, trial dates and recurring charge in checkout before confirming. A yearly subscription is $70 for the year, rather than twelve $7 payments.

Workspace owners receive a confirmation when the trial starts and a reminder three days before it ends. The reminder explains whether the saved card will be charged or renewal has been canceled. Billing emails are separate from feedback notification preferences.

## Pay once with local methods or crypto

After your trial has been used, **Settings → Billing** offers a one-time payment for the selected monthly/yearly access period. Bachs opens directly and handles available currencies and payment methods. Eligible new accounts use the card-only trial checkout; there is no extra Ent payment-choice dialog. For an existing prepaid plan, select **Extend Essentials**.

Prepaid access has no free trial or automatic renewal. It starts after payment is verified. Renew manually to extend the remaining access; you will not lose unused prepaid time. Card subscriptions and free trials still use the USD card rail. Local or international card acceptance depends on the issuer allowing that transaction.

If a card subscription is active, cancel its renewal and wait for its access period to end before switching to prepaid. This avoids overlapping charges. Crypto underpayments do not activate Essentials until Bachs confirms the complete payment.

## Manage or cancel

Open **Settings → Billing → Manage subscription** to manage Essentials in the app. The current billing period is marked in the monthly/yearly dropdown. Select a different period and save to schedule it for the next renewal; there is no charge today.

To stop renewals, select **Cancel subscription**, review the access end date, and confirm. Essentials remains available through the paid period or remaining trial. Existing feedback stays readable afterward.

If a request is not confirmed, use **Refresh subscription** to check the latest billing state before trying again.

An overlay returning successfully is not the final subscription confirmation. Ent updates access after the billing provider confirms the subscription. If that confirmation is delayed, return to Billing and refresh before trying another purchase.

## After a downgrade

Existing feedback remains readable. Free board limits apply to creating additional boards, and the Ent attribution badge returns when Essentials access ends. A downgrade does not automatically delete your collected feedback.

[Contact support](/support) for billing questions. Include enough information to find the workspace or subscription, but never send card details.
