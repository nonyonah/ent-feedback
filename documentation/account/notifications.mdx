title: "Notifications"
description: "Choose which activity you hear about."
---

Select the **bell icon** to open notifications. Use the settings control at the top right of that panel to change preferences.

You can control:

- New feedback.
- Comments on feedback.
- Replies to comments.
- Reactions to comments.
- Reactions to feedback.

Save your changes before leaving the preference view. A post’s **Follow this post** action and your relationship to the conversation also affect which updates are relevant to you. Enabling a category does not subscribe you to every workspace event.

## Missing an email

Check the email attached to your account, your preferences and your spam folder. Email delivery can depend on the workspace/service configuration; an in-app notification does not guarantee an email has reached your inbox.

If you contact support, include the activity type and approximate time. Do not send your password or authentication codes.
