## Public feedback

Public posts, display names, profile images, comments and attached images can be visible to other visitors. Do not submit confidential information. An email supplied with feedback is not shown as public post content.

## Optional product analytics

Ent asks before enabling PostHog usage analytics. Choose **Allow analytics** or **No thanks**. You can change that browser’s choice using **Analytics preferences** in the website footer, the Privacy notice or **Settings → Account → Privacy**.

Analytics records named product actions, such as creating a board or submitting feedback. It does not send feedback text, names, emails, attachments or raw URL queries. Automatic interaction capture and session recording are disabled.

When you are signed in and have allowed analytics, a hashed account identifier connects activity across devices. Signed-out activity uses a browser identifier. Signing out or switching accounts resets the browser identity. These identifiers are pseudonymous, not a claim that the data is fully anonymous.

Browser privacy signals keep analytics off by default. You can explicitly allow analytics in the card; a newly enabled privacy signal blocks collection again until acknowledged. Embedded widgets do not load Ent’s product analytics on the host website.
