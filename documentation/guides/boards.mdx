Choose **All boards** in the dashboard to see the boards in your current workspace. Each card shows its name and selected color dot or emoji.

## Create a board

Use the create-board button in the dashboard. Pick a color or emoji, choose a starter template under the name field, enter a name, then choose **Create**. You do not need to create another project or workspace first.

Free workspaces can have **two boards**. Essentials workspaces can have **six boards**. If you reach the limit, open [Billing](/account/billing) to review the current plan.

## Manage your boards

- Choose **Open inbox** on a card to view that board’s feedback.
- Drag board cards to reorder them.
- Open the card’s three-dot menu to rename it, duplicate it, copy its link, share it or view its public board.

## Start from a template

The create-board form offers four starter templates. **SaaS product** is selected by default.

| Template | Sample items |
| --- | --- |
| SaaS product | A welcome post plus common requests such as dark mode, data export and single sign-on |
| Mobile app | Requests such as offline mode, push notification settings and a launch crash fix |
| Developer tool | Requests such as CLI autocomplete, TypeScript types and webhooks |
| Blank | No items |

Sample items carry a **Sample** badge and a starting vote count. The starting count is added to real votes everywhere votes appear, including the board, search, the roadmap and the widget. Samples appear on the roadmap in their status column, so planned, in-progress and shipped samples reach the public roadmap. Visitors vote on them and reply in their conversation as they would on any other post.

## Clear sample content

Workspace owners see a banner above the board’s request list and a **Clear sample content** button on the board card in **All boards**. Confirm to remove every sample item on that board, including its votes and comments. Regular feedback stays, and clearing cannot be undone.

To keep one sample, open it, choose **Edit sample**, and save a new title or description. The item becomes ordinary feedback: its starting votes are removed, and the real votes and comments it already has stay.

## Duplicate a board

Workspace owners and admins can open a board’s three-dot menu and choose **Duplicate board**. The name field starts as the current name followed by **(copy)**; edit it to anything up to 60 characters, then choose **Duplicate board** to create it and open the new board.

**Copy items** is off by default. Tick it to bring the board’s feedback across, then tick **Copy votes and comments** underneath to include vote totals and reply threads. Unticking **Copy items** clears that second box.

The copy keeps the board’s description, color or emoji, and widget settings. Copied items keep their title, description, category, status, roadmap visibility and public note, so planned and shipped items arrive in the same columns. Merged and deleted items stay behind. The copy never carries over authors, attachments, integrations, keys or secrets, so no visitor identity reaches the new board. Copied votes become starting counts rather than linked voters. A duplicated board uses a board slot on your current plan like any other board.

## Check your current board

Status counts and request lists belong to the selected board or workspace view. When sharing a link or installing a widget, use the values generated for the board you want to collect feedback on.

## Delete a board

Workspace owners and admins can open **All boards**, choose the board’s three-dot menu, then select **Delete board**. Confirm the deletion in the dialog.

Deleting a board permanently removes its feedback, comments and votes. Its public link and widgets installed for that board stop working. Board counts and billing usage update, freeing a board slot on your current plan. You can also delete the last board and create a new one later.

## Check existing feedback before posting

Submitting feedback opens a search step first. Search the workspace’s feedback, bug reports and feature requests. Open a matching post to vote or comment, or choose **Create a new post** after checking the results. Identical titles are blocked across the workspace, including differences in capitalization or spacing.
