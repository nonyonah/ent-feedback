Choose **All boards** in the dashboard to see the boards in your current workspace. Each card shows its name and selected color dot or emoji.

## Create a board

Use the create-board button in the dashboard. Pick a color or emoji, enter a name, then choose **Create**. You do not need to create another project or workspace first.

Free workspaces can have **two boards**. Essentials workspaces can have **five boards**. If you reach the limit, open [Billing](/account/billing) to review the current plan.

## Manage your boards

- Choose **Open inbox** on a card to view that board’s feedback.
- Drag board cards to reorder them.
- Open the card’s three-dot menu to rename it, copy its link, share it or view its public board.

## Check your current board

Status counts and request lists belong to the selected board or workspace view. When sharing a link or installing a widget, use the values generated for the board you want to collect feedback on.
