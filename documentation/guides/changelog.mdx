---
title: Changelog
description: Publish what shipped and keep a public history of updates.
---

A changelog is a list of updates you write for the people who use your product. Each entry has a title, a date and a body, and every published entry appears on your workspace’s public Changelog page.

## Write an entry

Open **Changelog** under **Product updates** in the dashboard sidebar and choose **New entry**. Give the entry a **Title** of up to 120 characters and **Details** of up to 5,000 characters.

Details are plain text. Leave a blank line between paragraphs, and the public page splits the body there. Lines within one paragraph wrap together.

Choose **Save draft** to keep working later, or **Publish** to send it live. The form keeps your copy private until you publish it.

## Publish and unpublish

Every entry in the dashboard carries a **Published** badge with its date, or a **Draft** badge. Use the eye button on an entry to publish it, and the crossed-out eye button to move a published entry back to a draft.

Drafts never appear publicly. Unpublishing removes an entry from the public page and the header link until you publish something again. Re-publishing an entry keeps its original date, so an entry does not jump to the top of the list after an edit.

## Read it on your public site

Published entries appear newest first on your public page at `/p/<workspace>/changelog`, where `<workspace>` is your workspace handle. Workspaces with their own domain serve the same page at `/changelog`.

Each entry shows its title, its publish date and its paragraphs. Until you publish the first entry, the page shows an empty changelog, and the **Changelog** link in your public header stays hidden. That link appears once at least one entry is published.

## Edit and delete

Select the pencil button on an entry to edit its title or details, then choose **Save changes**. Edits reach the public page immediately.

Select the trash button to delete an entry. Deleting is permanent and also removes any published copy from your public changelog, so the confirmation dialog asks before it goes.

## Who can write entries

Workspace owners and admins can write, publish, unpublish and delete entries. Other workspace members can read the list in the dashboard but do not see the entry controls.
