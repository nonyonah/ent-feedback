---

Open a feedback card to read its full description, attachments, status and conversation.

## Comment or reply

Use the comment box to add context. Choose **Reply** under a comment to open a reply box for that conversation. The composer supports emoji and image attachments where shown.

If sending fails, your draft stays in the composer. Read the error and retry instead of starting over. A successful send clears the draft.

## Vote and react

Use the vote control on a post to support it. Select it again to remove your vote. Active votes use the workspace’s primary color.

The heart under a comment reacts to that comment. It is separate from voting on the post.

<Note>
Guest activity uses a browser identity. To compare your active votes across devices or between the dashboard and public board, sign in with the same account. Guest votes are not automatically the same as your signed-in votes.
</Note>

## Follow and share

In the post’s actions, choose **Follow this post**, **Copy link** or **Share**. Following keeps the post connected to your notification preferences. [Manage notifications](/account/notifications).

## Read status history

Status updates appear in the conversation timeline. This history is separate from comments and shows how the post has progressed.
