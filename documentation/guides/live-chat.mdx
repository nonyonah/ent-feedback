## Turn on chat

Open **Settings → Chat**, enable chat and save. The widget shows **Feedback** and **Live chat** in its bottom navigation. Visitors can switch between them without losing their message draft.

<Note>
AI replies are currently paused. Your team handles conversations. Website and documentation knowledge controls are disabled until AI replies are enabled. Saved sources are retained.
</Note>

## Find a conversation

The dashboard’s **Conversations** section has three views:

- **Queue:** conversations waiting for your team.
- **Open:** conversations in progress.
- **All:** every conversation, including resolved ones.

Search by message, visitor name or email, topic or status. Each row shows the visitor, latest message, relative time and status. Timestamps show Now, minutes, hours and then days.

## Reply and resolve

Select a conversation and write in the reply box at the bottom. Use the emoji and attachment controls to add context. You can attach up to three images, each no larger than 2 MB. Supported image formats are PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP.

On desktop, Enter sends and Shift+Enter adds a new line. If a send fails, the draft remains available to retry. Load failures show a recovery notice with a retry action.

Choose **Resolve** when you are done. A new message reopens the conversation. You can also manage the status from [Slack](/integrations/slack).

## Availability and operators

In **Settings → Chat**, choose your team’s availability, timezone, working days and hours, and away message. Choose all workspace members or select existing members as operators.

The widget shows a green dot and **Online now** when a selected teammate is active in Ent or Slack reports them as active. Your availability schedule still applies. App presence expires after heartbeats stop; Slack presence is cached briefly and refreshed while visitors have the widget open. Unknown or expired presence is not shown as online. Visitors can leave a message while nobody is online. Slack matching uses the teammate’s Ent email address.

## Continue after a refresh

The widget saves a workspace-specific conversation reference in the visitor’s browser. Refreshing the page or closing and reopening the widget reloads the conversation history. Choosing **New chat** starts a separate conversation.

Clearing site data, using a private session or switching browsers/devices can prevent restoration. Separate host websites may have separate browser storage; cross-site session transfer is not provided.

Visitors can optionally leave an email address for the team’s reply. They do not need an Ent account to start a chat. Signing in to your host application does not automatically identify them to Ent.
