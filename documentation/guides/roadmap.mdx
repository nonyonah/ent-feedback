---
title: Roadmap
description: Show users what is planned, in progress and shipped.
---

Your roadmap shows existing feedback grouped into **Planned**, **In Progress** and **Shipped**. Users can open each post to vote or join its conversation.

## View the public roadmap

Choose **Roadmap** beside Feedback on your public board. Phones show one column at a time. Shipped shows the last 30 days; choose **See all shipped** for earlier items.

The widget also includes a Roadmap tab. Its Shipped section shows the five most recent items.

## Manage the roadmap

Workspace owners can open **Roadmap** in the dashboard sidebar.

- New feedback appears in the Open column automatically. Drag an Open item into Planned when you are ready to share it.
- Drag cards between columns to change their status. Drag within a column to change their order. On phones, use the card’s status dropdown.
- Use the eye button to hide or show a card on the public roadmap.
- Add a short public note, up to 140 characters, then choose **Save**.
- Choose **Preview public roadmap** to open the public page.

Hidden cards remain visible to the owner with a faded appearance. Hiding a card removes it from the roadmap; its feedback post remains available on the board.

## Tell voters when an item ships

Moving a card to Shipped opens a dialog for an optional public note. Tick **Email people who voted** to send a shipped update. Leave it unticked to update the roadmap without that email.

Eligible voters receive at most one shipped email for that item. Delivery follows their notification preferences and workspace unsubscribe choice. Anonymous voters need a verified email. Emails are queued, so the status change saves before delivery finishes.

Public roadmap changes can take up to a minute to refresh.

### Organize open ideas

Owners can use the Open column in the dashboard roadmap to organize incoming ideas before planning them. Open items stay off the public roadmap and widget. Drag a card to change its status, or select its title to edit its status, visibility and public note. On phones, use the status dropdown in that dialog. Find Roadmap under **Product updates** in the dashboard sidebar.

### Collapse a column

Select a status heading to collapse or expand its cards, including in the widget. Counts remain visible. Collapsing changes only your current view and does not hide items from other visitors. Widget cards show the same public notes, voter avatars and totals as the public roadmap.
