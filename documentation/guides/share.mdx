---
title: "Share your board"
description: "Invite feedback with a link or an embedded widget."
---

Open **Share** in the dashboard while the intended workspace and board are selected.

## Copy a public link

Copy the feedback link from the share card. Open it in another tab to verify the workspace name and board before sharing it with users. **Preview** lets you inspect the public experience.

Visitors can read feedback, vote and join conversations through the board. Signing in from the public board returns them to the public experience rather than forcing product setup.

## Share on social platforms

Use the available X, Facebook, Reddit or LinkedIn actions to open that service’s sharing flow. Review the message before publishing it.

## Keep feedback in your app

Use **Widget embed** to copy an installation snippet. The widget opens from a floating **Submit feedback** launcher at the bottom right of your website.

[Install the widget](/widget/install).
