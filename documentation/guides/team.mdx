title: "Invite your team"
description: "Bring teammates into your workspace to review feedback and reply to live chat."
---

## Send an invitation

1. Open **Settings → Team** in the workspace you want to share.
2. Select **Invite team**.
3. Enter up to 10 email addresses, separated by commas.
4. Select **Send invitations**.

Existing Ent users sign in with their invited email. New teammates create an account, add their name if needed, and accept the invitation. They join your workspace without creating another one.

The invitation opens the joined workspace after acceptance. If the recipient is signed in with a different email, they must switch accounts first.

Invitations expire after seven days. Access starts only after acceptance; knowing an email address does not add someone automatically.

## Invite during onboarding

After creating a workspace, **Invite your team** is the final optional setup step. Enter email addresses and select **Send invitations**, or choose **Skip for now**. It uses the same invitation service and seven-day links as Settings → Team.

## What members can do

Members can review feedback. They can reply to live chats when selected as operators. They also appear in the operator selector in **Settings → Conversations**. Leave **All workspace members** on, or switch it off and select individual teammates. Save your changes. Selected operators can reply to live chat from Ent or Slack, appear online in the widget when available, and receive handoff emails. No selected operators means visitors can leave messages for later. Removing a teammate revokes their access and removes them from the selection. Workspace owners manage invitations and membership. Custom roles and role editing remain unavailable.

Slack replies must come from an active workspace member whose Slack email matches their Ent account. Live-chat replies and status actions also require selection as an operator; board replies do not.

## Manage invitations

Pending invitations appear below the member list, along with email delivery status. Cancel an invitation to invalidate its link. To send another invitation, cancel the pending one and invite that address again. An expired invitation can be replaced directly.

If email delivery fails, the invitation remains pending and the app shows a warning. Cancel it and send a fresh invitation after fixing delivery.

## Remove a teammate

Select **Remove** next to a member, then confirm. They lose workspace access; their existing feedback and replies remain. Re-invite them if they need to return. The workspace owner cannot be removed through this screen.
