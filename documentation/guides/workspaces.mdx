title: "Workspaces"
description: "Give each product its own home for feedback."
---

A workspace contains your boards, branding and collected feedback. Create boards inside a workspace rather than creating a new workspace for every topic.

## Create another workspace

Open the account or workspace switcher in the dashboard sidebar and choose **Create workspace**. Enter the name and workspace domain identifier. Wait for the availability checks to finish before submitting.

The domain identifier needs at least five characters. The form shows the resulting address and confirms available values with a checkmark. Your workspace name is the display name; the domain identifier is used in its address.

## Switch workspaces

Use the sidebar switcher to move between workspaces. Check the current workspace before creating a board or copying a feedback link.

## Change workspace branding

Open **Settings → Branding** to update the workspace name, image, primary color or public-board theme. Choose **Save changes** to apply your edits, or **Discard** to keep the saved settings.

A workspace image appears alongside its name on the public board and widget. If an image is missing, check that the upload completed and the branding changes were saved.

<Note>
Custom domains and team roles or permissions are not available in v1. The standard workspace address does not require either feature.
</Note>
