A workspace contains your boards, branding and collected feedback. Create boards inside a workspace rather than creating a new workspace for every topic.

## Create another workspace

Open the account or workspace switcher in the dashboard sidebar and choose **Create workspace**. Enter the name and workspace domain identifier. Wait for the availability checks to finish before submitting.

The domain identifier needs at least five characters. The form shows the resulting address and confirms available values with a checkmark. Your workspace name is the display name; the domain identifier is used in its address.

## Switch workspaces

Use the sidebar switcher to move between workspaces. Check the current workspace before creating a board or copying a feedback link.

## Share workspace access

Open **Settings → Team** to [invite teammates](/guides/team). They accept using the invited email address, whether they already use Ent or need to sign up. Accepting opens the joined workspace without creating another one.

## Change workspace branding

Open **Settings → Branding** to update the workspace name, image, primary color or public-board theme. Choose **Save changes** to apply your edits, or **Discard** to keep the saved settings.

A workspace image appears alongside its name on the public board and widget. If an image is missing, check that the upload completed and the branding changes were saved.

## Delete a workspace

Only a workspace owner can delete it. Deletion is permanent and removes the workspace for everyone, including its boards, feedback, conversations, changelog and memberships. Uploaded files are queued for removal. Data export is not currently available.

1. Open the workspace and go to **Settings → Branding → Workspace**.
2. Select **Delete workspace**, then type the workspace name exactly as shown.
3. Select **Delete workspace** in the confirmation dialog.

Before deleting, disconnect the workspace's integrations and end any active subscription or prepaid access. Canceling a subscription stops renewal; access may continue until the end of the current billing period. See [Plans and billing](/account/billing) for cancellation details.

A pending checkout also prevents deletion. Ent checks the billing provider before clearing an expired checkout; if a purchase completed, Ent reconciles it and asks you to review billing. If the provider cannot confirm the checkout or its details are unavailable, deletion remains blocked. Try again later or [contact support](/support) with the workspace details.

<Note>
Custom domains and custom team roles or permissions are not available in v1. The standard workspace address does not require either feature.
</Note>
