Ent brings feedback, bug reports and feature requests into one place. Share a public board or embed the widget in your app. Use the dashboard to read conversations, organize feedback and update its status.

## How Ent is organized

- **Workspace:** your product’s home, with its own name, image and public address.
- **Board:** a collection of related feedback inside a workspace.
- **Feedback type:** Feedback, Bug report or Feature request.
- **Status:** where a post stands, from Open to Shipped.
- **Tags:** labels you create to organize posts across topics.

## Available in v1

Both plans include unlimited feedback posts and voters. Free includes two boards and the Ent attribution badge. Essentials includes five boards and removes the badge. [Compare plans](/account/billing).

<Note>
Integrations, custom domains and team roles or permissions are not available in v1. Data export is also not available yet. A workspace’s standard Ent address is separate from a custom domain.
</Note>
