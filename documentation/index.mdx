Ent brings feedback, bug reports and feature requests into one place. Share a public board or embed the widget in your app. Use the dashboard to reply to live chats, organize feedback and update its status.

## How Ent is organized

- **Workspace:** your product’s home, with its own name, image and public address.
- **Board:** a collection of related feedback inside a workspace.
- **Feedback type:** Feedback, Bug report or Feature request.
- **Status:** where a post stands, from Open to Shipped.
- **Tags:** labels you create to organize posts across topics.

## Work with your team

[Invite teammates](/guides/team) during onboarding or from **Settings → Team**. Existing users sign in; new users create an account and accept the invitation. Choose which accepted members handle live chat in **Settings → Conversations**.

## Talk with your users

Enable [live chat](/guides/live-chat) in the widget and reply from Ent or [Slack](/integrations/slack). Set your team’s availability, choose operators and resolve conversations when they are done. AI replies are currently paused.

## Available in v1

Both plans include unlimited feedback posts and voters. Free includes two boards and the Ent attribution badge. Essentials includes six boards and removes the badge. [Compare plans](/account/billing).

<Note>
Slack is available for feedback and live chat, and [Linear](/integrations/linear) links feedback to issues. [GitHub](/integrations/github) can create private changelog drafts from merged pull requests. Discord, custom domains and custom team roles or permissions are not available yet. Data export is also not available yet. A workspace’s standard Ent address is separate from a custom domain.
</Note>
