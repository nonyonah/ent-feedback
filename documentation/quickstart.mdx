---
title: "Get started"
description: "Create a workspace, add a board and share it with your users."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Choose **Sign up** from Ent’s main website. Complete the authentication step, then enter your first name, last name and optional profile image during setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your workspace">
    Enter a workspace name and domain identifier. The identifier needs at least five characters and must be available. Ent checks the name and domain before creating the workspace. Use the address shown in the form rather than guessing its suffix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your boards">
    Finish setup to enter the dashboard. Choose **All boards** to see the workspace’s boards. If you need another board, use the create-board button, select a name and a color or emoji, then choose **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share a board">
    Open **Share**, copy the feedback link and open it in another tab. Submit a short test post, then return to the dashboard to check that it appears in the intended board.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose how to collect feedback

Share a [public board link](/guides/share) or [install the widget](/widget/install) on your website. Both send feedback to your workspace.

<Note>
Signing up from a public feedback board returns the visitor to that board. It does not require them to create a workspace. To manage a product of their own, they can open **My dashboard** and complete workspace setup.
</Note>
